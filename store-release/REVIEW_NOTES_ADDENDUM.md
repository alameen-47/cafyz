## Cafyz — append to App Review Information > Notes

Regarding the Guideline 4.3 and 4.2.6 questions of 6 October: full answers were sent in the App Review thread on 23 October, with Cafyz-App-Review-answers.pdf attached. That submission was replaced only so this build could be attached; the answers stand. In summary:

Cafyz is restaurant floor-and-kitchen software: a waiter's order terminal, a kitchen display, live table state, bill splitting and thermal receipt printing. It is the only app on this account that takes a restaurant order or drives a kitchen display.

The other apps on this account serve different trades and share no significant code with this one: Ametronyx (freelancer projects and invoices), AxPro ERP (business management, Saudi Arabia), Coorg Trade (produce buying by weight for Kodagu estates), CREDI8 (shop credit ledger and payment reminders), FitCore Plus (gym management with a member app, approved 23 October). None has a menu, a table, an order ticket or a kitchen screen, so this functionality could not be delivered as an in-app purchase inside any of them.

Cafyz was not built from a commercial template or an app-generation service, and was not built for a client. It is our own product, with its own repository, data model and interface.

Build 1.0 (3) raises the network timeout so the app waits for a cold server to answer instead of showing a connection error.

## Coorg Trade — append to App Review Information > Notes

Regarding the Guideline 4.3 and 4.2.6 questions of 6 October: full answers were sent in the App Review thread on 23 October, with Coorg-Trade-App-Review-answers.pdf attached. That submission was replaced only so this build could be attached; the answers stand. In summary:

Coorg Trade is a buying ledger for produce traders in Kodagu, Karnataka: purchases recorded by gross and tare weight with a moisture cut and coffee outturn, rates per bag or quintal, and grower advances carried against future deliveries. No other app on this account weighs produce or computes an outturn.

The other apps on this account serve different trades and share no significant code with this one: Ametronyx (freelancer projects and invoices), AxPro ERP (business management, Saudi Arabia), Cafyz (restaurant point of sale and kitchen display), CREDI8 (shop credit ledger and payment reminders), FitCore Plus (gym management with a member app, approved 23 October). None records a weight-based purchase or a grower advance, so this functionality could not be delivered as an in-app purchase inside any of them.

Coorg Trade was not built from a commercial template or an app-generation service, and was not built for a client. It is our own product, with its own repository, data model and interface.

Build 1.0 (6) raises the network timeout so the app waits for a cold server to answer instead of showing a connection error.
